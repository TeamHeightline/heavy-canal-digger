Tracked Canal Digger 0.5.3 — Factorio 2.0

Choose Travel or Work in the vehicle window. Travel raises the cutting wheel
and allows up to 30 km/h. Work lowers the wheel and limits speed to 3 km/h.
Work mode turns five times more slowly. R rotates placement previews and
parked machines. The cutting wheel rotates with movement in Work mode.
Excavation converts ground into a nominally 5.75-tile-wide strip of actual water for
Cargo Ships. Ships need wider basins at bends. Buildings, plants, rocks
and cliffs block work and are never removed by the digging script.
Ground decoratives are removed from converted water, including water near
existing vehicles on upgrade. Dry-land decoratives and real trees are retained.

Each new water tile produces one Soil in the 80-slot trunk. Full cargo or
empty fuel pauses work. Soil can be converted to landfill: 20 soil per item.
The chassis consumes 600 kW; digging uses an additional 1.8 MW while working.
The vehicle cannot drive on any water, including its own channel. Reverse
over dry land for continuous digging; forward motion stops at the water edge.
Ground beneath the vehicle is protected. Water excavation is Nauvis-only.
Unsafe turns at a water edge are rejected while dry-land escape stays possible.

Unlocked by Railway alongside the locomotive. Recipe: 100 steel plates,
80 iron gears, 20 engine units, 40 electronic circuits and 100 iron plates.
Crafting time: 30 seconds. Fuel inventory: 3 slots, chemical fuel.

Both supplied USDZ models are rendered in 64 directions; work has four
wheel phases. Lighting now matches the final dark-metal studio settings.
Mode switching preserves the full inventories, occupants, health and fuel.

Copy the ZIP, without extracting, to %APPDATA%\Factorio\mods.
Final in-game testing is reserved for the user.
